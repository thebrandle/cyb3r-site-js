Your buyers are asking AI who to hire

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews for a recommendation before they ever visit a website. If your brand is missing from those answers, you can lose the deal without knowing it existed. As an AI SEO agency based in the UK, with a team in London, we help you show up in that conversation.

You will see this work called generative engine optimisation (GEO), answer engine optimisation (AEO) or AI search optimisation. The names differ, but the goal is the same: when a buyer asks an AI tool about your category, your business is named, described correctly and linked.

How our GEO work runs

We start with an AI visibility audit. We run the questions your buyers really ask across the main AI tools and record who gets mentioned, what is said about you and which sources are cited. That shows where the gaps are and what your competitors are doing better.

Then we fix the causes. We open your site to AI crawlers, add schema and an llms.txt file, rewrite key pages so they answer questions directly, and earn mentions on the sites these tools trust. Good GEO is built on good SEO, so the same work helps you in normal Google results too.

What we can and cannot promise

Nobody controls what an AI model says, and any agency promising a guaranteed mention is overselling. What we can do is improve the signals these tools rely on, then measure the change: how often you are mentioned against competitors, and how much traffic and how many leads arrive from AI search. As a GEO agency that also runs SEO, content and web design, we can make the changes ourselves.

Questions we get asked

What is generative engine optimisation?

Generative engine optimisation, or GEO, is the work of making your brand visible in AI-generated answers from tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO aims to rank your pages in search results. GEO aims to get your brand mentioned and cited inside AI answers. They share the same foundations, and strong SEO helps GEO.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Make sure AI crawlers can read your site, explain clearly what you do and who for, answer real buyer questions, and earn mentions and reviews on trusted third-party sites.

How long does AI search optimisation take?

Technical fixes can land quickly, but AI answers change at their own pace. Expect to review progress over several months rather than weeks, and we report on it regularly.

Do I still need SEO if I do GEO?

Yes. Many AI tools draw on search results, so pages that rank well are more likely to be used. The two work best as one programme.
