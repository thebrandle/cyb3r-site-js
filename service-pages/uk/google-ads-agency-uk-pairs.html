A UK PPC agency that decides what not to pay for

Google Ads is easy to start and easy to waste. A campaign left on broad settings can spend a month of budget on people who were never going to enquire. As a PPC agency based in the UK, with a team in London, we build accounts around intent and treat your budget like our own.

That means separating people ready to buy from people still researching, writing ads for each, and sending them to pages that finish the job. Whether you need full PPC management or a Google Ads consultant to review what you already run, the aim is the same: more of the right leads for less.

How our Google Ads management works

We start with a Google Ads audit, or set your account up correctly, then fix tracking before spending more. Without clean conversion data, Google optimises for the wrong thing. Once calls, forms and sales are measured, we structure paid search campaigns around your most profitable services.

From there it is hands-on work every week: search terms, bids, budgets, audiences and new creative. You see the same numbers we see, and you own your account and your data.

Questions we get asked

What is a PPC agency?

A PPC agency plans, runs and improves pay-per-click advertising, such as Google Ads, on your behalf. You pay the platform for clicks, and the agency manages the campaigns so that spend turns into leads and sales.

How much should I spend on Google Ads in the UK?

It depends on your sector, your target areas and what a lead is worth to you. We look at likely click costs first, then recommend a budget that can produce enough data to work.

How much does PPC management cost?

Your ad spend goes directly to Google, and our management fee is agreed up front based on the size of the account and the work involved.

How quickly will I see results?

Ads can bring in enquiries within days of launch, once tracking is live. Expect the first weeks to be about learning, with performance improving as we cut waste.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

Yes. The account, the data and the campaign history stay yours. If we ever part ways, everything we built stays with you.
