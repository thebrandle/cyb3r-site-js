Standing out in a market that looks the same

Dubai is full of polished brands. Walk through any business district and you will see the same gold, the same skyline and the same promises of luxury. When everyone looks premium, premium stops being a difference. A strong brand here needs a clear idea of what it stands for, and the discipline to say it the same way everywhere.

That matters more in a market where new companies launch every week and audiences come from all over the world. Your brand has to make sense quickly, to very different people, in more than one language.

How we build your brand

We begin with strategy: your market, your competitors and the position you can credibly own. Only then do we move to design. Logo, type, colour, imagery and tone of voice are built as one system, so they work together on a website, in a pitch deck, on a stand and on a building.

You receive everything your team needs to use it well, including guidelines, master files and templates. If your brand needs an Arabic version, we design it alongside the English from the start, rather than adding it at the end.

From identity to launch

A brand only works once people see it. We work from hubs in London, Dubai and Cyprus, and our team has delivered work for brands including Dubai Shopping Festival, Dubai Economy and Tourism and Stable Rise. The same team can build your website, produce your print and run your launch campaigns, so the idea does not get diluted on its way to market.

Questions we get asked

How much does branding cost in Dubai?

It depends on the scope: a logo alone costs far less than full strategy, identity and guidelines. We give you a fixed quote after a discovery call.

How long does a branding project take?

A full identity usually takes a matter of weeks, covering strategy, design and guidelines. Rounds of feedback and the size of your team affect the timeline, and we agree dates up front.

Do you design Arabic logos?

Yes. We design Arabic and English logos together so they share the same character, rather than treating the Arabic as a translation.

What is the difference between a logo and a brand?

A logo is one mark. A brand is what people think and feel about you, shaped by your positioning, name, voice, visuals and how you behave. We can help with all of it.

Can you refresh our brand without starting again?

Yes. Many businesses need evolution, not revolution. We keep what people already recognise and fix what is holding you back.
