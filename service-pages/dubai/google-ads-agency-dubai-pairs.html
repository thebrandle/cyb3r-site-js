Paid search in a competitive market

Clicks in Dubai are expensive in sectors such as property, finance and healthcare, because plenty of well-funded advertisers want the same buyers. A campaign left on broad settings can spend a month of budget on people who were never going to enquire. Good management is mostly about deciding what not to pay for.

We build accounts around intent. That means separating people ready to buy from people still researching, writing ads for each, and sending them to pages that finish the job. It also means planning for the way your audience searches, in English and Arabic.

How we run your Google Ads

We start by auditing your account, or setting one up correctly, then fix tracking before spending more. Without clean conversion data, Google optimises for the wrong thing. Once calls, forms and WhatsApp clicks are measured, we structure campaigns around your most profitable services.

From there it is hands-on work every week: search terms, bids, budgets, audiences and new creative. You see the same numbers we see, and you own your account and your data.

Ads, pages and creative under one roof

Most wasted ad spend is lost after the click, on slow or unclear pages. Our team builds the landing pages and the creative as well as the campaigns, working from hubs in London, Dubai and Cyprus. For display, our Relevance Advertising approach matches your message to the page it appears on, instead of chasing cookies around the web.

Questions we get asked

How much should I spend on Google Ads in Dubai?

It depends on your sector, your target areas and what a lead is worth to you. We look at likely click costs for your terms first, then recommend a budget that can produce enough data to work.

How quickly will I see results?

Ads can bring in enquiries within days of launch, once tracking is live. Expect the first weeks to be about learning, with performance improving as we cut waste and build on what works.

Do you charge a management fee on top of ad spend?

Yes. Your ad spend goes directly to Google, and our management fee is agreed up front based on the size of the account and the work involved.

Will I own my Google Ads account?

Yes. The account, the data and the campaign history stay yours. If we ever part ways, everything we built stays with you.

Can you fix an account that is not performing?

Yes. We often take over existing accounts. An audit usually shows where the waste is, and tracking and campaign structure are the most common places to start.
