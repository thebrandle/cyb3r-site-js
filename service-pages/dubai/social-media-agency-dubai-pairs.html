Social is how Dubai discovers you

In the UAE, social media is often the first place people meet a brand and the place they go to check you are real. A restaurant, a developer or a clinic can be judged on its Instagram before anyone visits the website. Your feed needs to look the part, and it needs to be active.

The audience here is also very mixed, spanning Emirati, Arab, South Asian and Western communities, often on different platforms. What works on TikTok will not work on LinkedIn, and a direct translation rarely lands. Good social in Dubai starts by knowing exactly who you are speaking to.

How we run your social media

We begin with a strategy and an audit of your channels and competitors, then build content pillars and a calendar you approve in advance. Our team handles the planning, shooting, editing, writing and posting, so your feed stays consistent without taking over your week.

We then manage the conversation: comments, messages and enquiries, handled in your voice. Paid social puts your strongest posts in front of new people, and every month you see what worked and what we are changing.

Content that connects to the rest of your marketing

Followers alone do not pay the bills. Because the same team handles your brand, website and ads, working from hubs in London, Dubai and Cyprus, social is connected to the rest of your marketing. Good posts become ads, ads lead to pages that convert, and you can see how social contributes to real enquiries.

Questions we get asked

How much does social media management cost in Dubai?

It depends on the number of platforms, how much content you need and whether filming is included. We build a monthly package around your goals and give you a clear fixed fee.

Which platforms should my business use?

It depends on your buyers. Instagram and TikTok suit most consumer brands in the UAE, LinkedIn suits B2B, and Snapchat can be strong for younger local audiences. We recommend the few that will do the most for you.

Do you create content in Arabic?

Yes. We write for English and Arabic audiences, and we adapt ideas for each rather than translating captions line by line.

Do you film on location in Dubai?

Yes. We plan and produce content shoots around your business, and we can also turn the footage you already have into a steady flow of posts.

How do you measure social media results?

We look past likes at reach among the right people, saves, shares, profile visits and enquiries. Our monthly reports show what each piece of content contributed.
