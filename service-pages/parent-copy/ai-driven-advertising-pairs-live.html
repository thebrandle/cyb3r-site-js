AI advertising that still has a person in charge

Google Ads now runs on AI. Smart Bidding sets every bid, Performance Max chooses where your ads appear, and AI Max for Search matches your ads to searches you never typed as keywords. Used well, these tools find customers faster than any manual setup. Left alone, they spend your budget on whatever is easiest to buy.

As an AI advertising agency in London and Dubai, we use Google's AI where it is strong and keep people in charge where it is not. The machine handles bids and placements in real time. We decide what it should learn from, what it is not allowed to do, and when the results do not add up.

How we run AI-powered Google Ads

Every account starts with tracking. AI bidding only optimises for what it can measure, so we set up conversion tracking and GA4 first, and feed back the leads and sales that actually turned into revenue. Without that, Google learns to chase cheap clicks.

Then we build campaigns the AI can work with: Search with AI Max, Performance Max, Demand Gen and YouTube, each with clear budgets, audience signals from your first-party data, and brand rules. AI ad creative gives us more headlines, images and video variations to test, and every one is checked by a person before it goes live.

Each week we read the search terms, placements and asset reports, cut waste, and move budget to what is working. You see the same numbers we see, and you own your account and your data.

Who AI-driven advertising is for

Ecommerce brands use Performance Max and Shopping to scale sales across Google. Lead generation businesses use AI bidding to find more enquiries at a steady cost per lead. Brands launching something new use Demand Gen and YouTube to build an audience before people search. For display, our Relevance Advertising approach places your message next to content that matches it, instead of following people around the web with cookies.

Questions we get asked

What is AI advertising?

AI advertising uses machine learning to decide who sees an ad, where it appears, what it says and how much to bid. Google Ads, Meta and YouTube all use it. An agency sets the goals, data and limits that the AI works within.

Does Google Ads use AI?

Yes. Smart Bidding, Performance Max, Demand Gen and AI Max for Search all use Google AI to set bids, choose placements, match searches and build ads from your assets.

How much does AI advertising cost?

Your ad spend goes directly to Google, and our management fee is agreed up front based on the size of the account and the work involved.

How quickly does AI bidding work?

New campaigns go through a learning period while Google gathers conversion data. Ads can bring in enquiries within days, and performance usually steadies over the first few weeks as the AI learns and we cut waste.

Will I lose control of my account with AI campaigns?

No. We set budgets, brand exclusions, audience signals and creative rules, review the reports every week, and the account and its data stay yours.
